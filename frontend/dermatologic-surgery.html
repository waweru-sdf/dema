Dermatologic Surgery

Surgical procedures for benign and malignant conditions of the skin and skin appendages.

Excision of skin cancers

Removal of basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma and melanoma.

Mohs surgery

A precise technique for removing skin cancer with minimal damage to surrounding tissue.

Cyst and lipoma removal

Excision of benign growths such as cysts and lipomas.

Scar revision

Improving the appearance of scars from injuries or surgery.

Cosmetic procedures

Mole removal, skin resurfacing and corrective surgery for cosmetic enhancement.
