Cosmetic Dermatology

Non-surgical and minimally invasive treatments to refresh and rejuvenate your skin.

Botox

Purified botulinum toxin is injected into specific facial muscles to relax them temporarily, reducing crow's feet, frown lines and forehead lines.

Laser hair removal

Concentrated light beams target and destroy hair follicles, giving long-term hair reduction and smoother skin.

Cryolipolysis

Also known as CoolSculpting. Fat cells are frozen and destroyed without harming surrounding tissue, reducing localised fat.

HydraFacial

A multi-step facial that cleanses, exfoliates, extracts and hydrates, leaving the skin refreshed and glowing.

Microneedling (RF)

Fine needles combined with radiofrequency energy stimulate collagen and elastin, improving texture, firmness, scars and wrinkles.

Excimer laser

Ultraviolet light targets abnormal skin cells to treat psoriasis, vitiligo and atopic dermatitis.
