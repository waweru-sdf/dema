General Dermatology

Diagnosis and treatment of common and chronic conditions of the skin, hair and nails.

Common skin conditions

We manage conditions such as acne, eczema, psoriasis and rosacea, as well as chronic skin diseases.

Infections and rashes

Treatment of bacterial, fungal, viral and parasitic infections, and allergic reactions such as contact dermatitis.

Skin cancer screening

Skin checks to detect early signs of skin cancer, including melanoma.

Pigment disorders and laser

Management of vitiligo and melasma, including excimer laser treatment.

Hair and nail issues

Care for hair loss, fungal infections and nail abnormalities.
