About us

Healthy skin is part of overall health

At Dr. Feysel Specialty Dermatology Clinic, we understand that healthy skin is not just about appearance. It is about overall health. Our team of experienced dermatologists is committed to providing top-notch, patient-centred care.

We diagnose and treat a wide range of dermatological conditions, so you receive personal attention and effective solutions. Whether you need treatment for a specific skin condition or are considering a cosmetic procedure, we have the expertise to address your needs.

Our services

Care for every skin concern

We treat issues of the skin, hair and nails, including dandruff, dermatitis, photoaging, rashes, rosacea, skin tags, skin cancer and warts.

General Dermatology

Treatment of conditions affecting the skin, hair and nails, including vitiligo and psoriasis with excimer laser.

Learn more

Dermatologic Surgery

Surgical procedures for benign and malignant conditions of the skin and skin appendages, including excisional and incisional biopsy.

Learn more

Cosmetic Dermatology

Botox, laser hair removal, cryolipolysis, HydraFacial, microneedling and excimer laser treatments.
